Totto-chan: The Little Girl at the Window

Tetsuko Kuroyanagi

This has been my favorite book since I first read it at age 8. A memoir which captures the intensity of emotion that only a child can feel, but eloquently worded the way only someone with decades of life experience can do. I'm pretty sure this book is also why I care so deeply about education, and teaching. My comfort book, and my biggest recommendation.
