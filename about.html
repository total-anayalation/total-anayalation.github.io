About

What happens when a wannabe teacher studies computer science and then discovers AI Safety.

Hi! I'm Anaya, an incoming third year at the University of Wisconsin–Madison studying Computer Science and Data Science, with a minor in Education Policy. I'm fascinated by how technology shapes institutions, and how institutions shape who gets access to technology.

I'm the current deputy director at UW's AI Safety organization (the Wisconsin AI Safety Initiative aka WAISI), where I spend a lot of time thinking about various risk areas in AI Safety, and how I as a generalist can help. I believe AI safety is one of the most important problems of our generation — and I think people with policy and social science backgrounds need to be in that conversation.

The intersection

In CS I love machine learning. In data science I gravitate toward causal inference and fairness. In education policy I care about how evidence actually (or doesn't) make it into decisions. Put those together and you get someone who really enjoys a good messy problem.

  • University of Wisconsin–Madison — Computer Science and Data Science, with a minor in Education Policy. Rising third year.
  • Wisconsin AI Safety Initiative (WAISI) — deputy director. Reading groups, research discussions, and building a community of people thinking carefully about advanced AI.

Outside class

When I'm not studying or at an AI safety reading group, I'm usually either cooking up some new recipe, hanging out with friends, or listening to music. I also like singing, making papercrafts, and talking to strangers. I believe in warm conversations, intellectual honesty, and the power of a good question.
